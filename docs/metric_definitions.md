# Executive KPI definitions

**TDAP reported export value** — sum of reported export value in the loaded TDAP extract; not a reconciled national total.

**Unique exporters** — distinct exporter entities observed in the loaded extract.

**Distinct HS8 products** — distinct 8-digit HS codes represented in the extract.

**Top 10 exporters' share** — percentage of extract value represented by the ten largest exporters.

**Exporters needed for 60%** — minimum number of exporters, ranked by reported value, needed to reach at least 60% cumulative extract value.

**HHI concentration index** — Herfindahl-Hirschman Index calculated from exporter shares in the extract; higher values imply greater concentration.
